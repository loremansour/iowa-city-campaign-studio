About the studio

An Iowa City creative studio built around focused campaigns.

Iowa City Campaign Studio is a fictional campaign strategy and creative studio serving local and regional businesses. Our approach brings audience research, messaging, content production, and launch planning into one coordinated project.

A specific goal gives the work direction.

A business introducing a service needs to explain its value. A community event needs to give people a reason to attend. A brand reaching a new audience needs to understand what matters to that audience.

Our studio concept centers on these defined moments. Each campaign begins with a brief that identifies the goal, audience, timeline, and intended action. That brief guides the creative idea and the materials developed for launch.

The project-based format gives each engagement a defined scope and handoff. Deliverables might include a campaign concept, photography, short-form video, social media assets, and a publishing and measurement plan.

How we work

Three principles behind each project.

Understand the audience.

Identify who the campaign should reach, what they need to know, and what might stop them from taking the next step. Use those insights to shape the message.

Connect every piece.

Give photography, video, captions, and landing-page messaging a shared direction. Adapt each asset to its channel while keeping the campaign recognizable.

Define success early.

Choose measures that reflect the campaign goal. Record a starting point, review results, and use the evidence to guide the next round of decisions.

Designed for local and regional business needs.

The studio's fictional service area centers on Iowa City and nearby communities. Example projects include introducing a fitness class, promoting a makers market, or helping a small business explain a new offering.

Our sample work illustrates how these projects could be approached. It does not represent real clients, completed engagements, or proven business results.
