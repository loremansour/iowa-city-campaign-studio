Our services

Campaign strategy and creative services in Iowa City.

A service launch, community event, or new audience needs a focused plan. Our project-based approach connects campaign strategy, creative production, and launch measurement around one goal.

Projects can combine all three stages or focus on a specific need. Scope depends on the audience, timeline, production requirements, and channels involved.

01 · Set the direction

Campaign strategy

Before creating content, define the action the campaign should encourage. We develop a campaign brief that connects your business goal with an audience, message, and creative concept.

For a small business introducing a new service, that might mean identifying the most relevant customers, explaining the service's value, and planning content that answers their questions.

What the plan includes

  • A defined campaign goal and intended audience.
  • Audience research and a review of competing messages.
  • A central message and supporting talking points.
  • A creative concept, moodboard, and channel recommendations.
  • A project timeline and agreed measures of success.

02 · Make the idea visible

Creative production

Turn the campaign concept into photography, short-form video, and social media assets that share a consistent visual direction. Each piece is planned for its audience and publishing format.

Production begins with a shot list and content outline so the finished materials support the campaign's message and next step.

What production can include

  • Creative direction and a photography shot list.
  • Brand, product, or service photography.
  • Short-form video concepts, scripts, and editing.
  • Social media graphics and caption drafts.
  • Asset versions adapted for selected channels.

03 · Publish with purpose

Launch planning and measurement

Organize the campaign into a publishing schedule with a clear call to action. Decide in advance which results will show whether people noticed the campaign and took the intended action.

An event campaign might track registration-page visits and completed registrations. A service launch might track visits to the service page and qualified project inquiries.

What the launch plan includes

  • A publishing calendar with channel-specific content.
  • Calls to action connected to the campaign goal.
  • Recommendations for campaign link tracking.
  • A measurement plan with defined reporting dates.
  • A review of results and suggested improvements.
