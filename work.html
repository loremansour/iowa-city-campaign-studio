Concept 01 · Service launch

Introducing beginner classes at Cedar Movement Studio.

Cedar Movement Studio is a fictional Iowa City fitness business introducing a beginner strength class. The campaign would help first-time participants understand what to expect and find information about booking an introductory session.

Creative direction

Your first rep starts here.

Use approachable photography, clear explanations, and instructor-led video to make the first class feel familiar. Show how a session works, including arrival, warm-up, instruction, and modifications for different abilities.

Audience and message

Audience: Iowa City adults interested in strength training who are unsure how to begin.

Message: Start with guided instruction and learn the basics one session at a time.

Call to action: View the beginner class schedule.

Content and rollout

  • A short video walking through a first class.
  • A carousel answering common beginner questions.
  • Instructor portraits with introductory captions.
  • A three-week sequence: introduce the class, explain the experience, then share booking information.

How we would measure it

Record class-page visits and introductory bookings before launch. Use tagged campaign links to identify visits from campaign posts. Review those visits and completed bookings weekly, then compare the campaign period with the baseline while noting other promotions that could affect demand.

Concept 02 · Event promotion

Building awareness for Riverlight Makers Market.

Riverlight Makers Market is a fictional community shopping event in Iowa City. The campaign would introduce participating makers and encourage local residents to view event details and register for a free reminder.

Creative direction

Meet the hands behind the goods.

Lead with the people and processes behind locally made products. Pair maker portraits with close-up production footage and clear event information so interest has an obvious next step.

Audience and message

Audience: Iowa City residents and students interested in local shopping and community events.

Message: Discover independent makers and learn the stories behind their work.

Call to action: View market details and sign up for a reminder.

Content and rollout

  • A series of maker spotlight videos.
  • Product photography and short maker interviews.
  • An event-information carousel with access details.
  • A four-week sequence: announce the market, introduce makers, share practical details, then publish reminders.

How we would measure it

Track event-page visits from tagged campaign links and completed reminder registrations. Review these weekly alongside post reach and engagement. If attendance is counted at the event, report it separately: online registrations alone do not establish attendance or prove that the campaign caused a visit.

Connect the creative to a clear goal.

These examples use different audiences and content, but share the same structure: define the goal, develop the message, plan the assets, and decide how progress will be measured.
